Help Desk

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Emergency Contacts.

SOP-01: Yellow Line Platform Dwell

When passengers dwell beyond the tactile warning line for > 3 seconds, automated PA audio plays. If passenger remains after 10 seconds, station staff are alerted to clear the edge.

SOP-04: Track Intrusion Protocol

Human or foreign object in track envelope triggers automated RED signals on approaching trains. Controller verifies video and authorizes emergency traction power cutoff if required.
